THE POTASSIUM DEFICIT VARIES DIRECTLY WITH THE SEVERITY OF HYPOKALEMIA. IN DIFFERENT STUDIES, THE SERUM POTASSIUM CONCENTRATION FELL BY APPROXIMATELY 0.27 MEQ/L FOR EVERY 100 MEQ REDUCTION IN TOTAL BODY POTASSIUM STORES [3,39,41] AND, IN CHRONIC HYPOKALEMIA, A POTASSIUM DEFICIT OF 200 TO 400 MEQ IS REQUIRED TO LOWER THE SERUM POTASSIUM CONCENTRATION BY 1 MEQ/L [41]. HOWEVER, THESE ESTIMATES ARE ONLY AN APPROXIMATION OF THE AMOUNT OF POTASSIUM REPLACEMENT REQUIRED TO NORMALIZE THE SERUM POTASSIUM CONCENTRATION AND CAREFUL MONITORING IS REQUIRED.
